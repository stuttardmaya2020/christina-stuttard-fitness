# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Referred newcomers: people sent a link by a friend or an existing client (word of mouth, WhatsApp, Instagram). They arrive already half-trusting Christina and want to see who she is, what she offers and how to start.

Christina trains people of every age and fitness level. Her clients are in Spain (in person) and around the world on Zoom; class times are published for the USA (Eastern), the UK and Spain.

## Product Purpose

The website for Christina Stuttard, personal trainer. It is a credibility page, not a lead-generation engine: new business comes from referral, and the site's job is to confirm to someone who has just heard of her that she is real, professional and worth messaging. Success is a referred visitor leaving with their good impression confirmed and the practical facts in hand (what she offers, when classes run, what they cost, how to reach her).

## Positioning

"I bring the gym to you." Christina creates all of her own routines and trains people where they are: at home in Spain, or live on Zoom anywhere in the world. Clients stay for the variety (functional strength, Pilates, sculpt; never the same class twice), for training shaped around their goals and schedule, and for a worldwide community of workout buddies.

## Operating Context

- Four ways to train: one-to-one at home in Spain, one-to-one on Zoom, small groups with friends, and live Zoom classes.
- Live Zoom classes are 30 minutes, Monday to Thursday, scheduled on US Eastern Time. Members need a mat and a set of dumbbells.
- Classes are drop-in, pay as you go: $15 per class (£12, €14).
- People get in touch by messaging Christina directly; she replies to every message herself. Email is the preferred channel (christinastuttardfitness@gmail.com), then WhatsApp (+34 633 39 77 28) and Instagram (@christinastuttard).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step. This is the current state, not a confirmed constraint.
- The page shows class times converted for the USA, UK and Spain (including clock changes), a class video, a quotes slider and an email copy button.
- Contact is by message only. No booking system, payments or forms.
- Class times (`startsET` in `main.js`) and prices (the `.price` block in `index.html`) are the things changed most often.
- Undecided: the live domain and hosting, and who maintains the site after launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Christina Stuttard Fitness (CSF), with the CSF logo at `images/csf-logo.png`.
- The wording is Christina's own: her bio, offer descriptions and taglines. Ask before rewriting it or adding claims.
- The logo and the Moira Lynch photography are the brand's imagery. No stock photos and no generated images of Christina.
- Colours: Christina likes the lemony yellow and grey.
- Voice, as it appears in her copy: first person, warm, energetic and direct ("Don't be afraid to ask", "Hello!"). She has been described as a "punky, spunky, uberfit instructor who loves to whip people into shape".

## Evidence on Hand

- Photography by Moira Lynch Photography, in `images/` (nine photos, credited in the footer).
- Four client testimonials on the page, attributed by first name and initial: Karen D., Janice B., Suzanne P., Tracy G.
- Christina's biography: BSc (Hons) in Biochemistry, London; moved to the United States in 2001 and began teaching fitness; moved to Spain in April 2021.
- A video of Christina teaching a class.
- Not on hand, and not to be invented: fitness certifications or qualifications, client numbers, results statistics, press coverage, and prices for one-to-one or small-group training.

## Product Principles

1. Confirm, don't persuade. The visitor was sent by someone they trust; show them the real Christina and let that do the work.
2. Christina speaks for herself. Her words and her photos carry the page; nothing is rewritten or added without asking her.
3. Practical facts are easy to find. Class times in the visitor's own time zone, the price and how to get in touch are never buried.
4. Every route ends in a conversation. The next step is always a message to Christina, never a form, a checkout or a booking flow.
